Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 3-5 days in advance for standard delivery. For custom orders or peak seasons (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day), please order 1-2 weeks ahead.

Can I include a custom message?

Absolutely! Every order includes a complimentary handwritten note. You can add your message during checkout, and our team will beautifully hand-write it on a story-style card.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes, we ship to all 50 states. Delivery times vary by location, typically 1-3 business days for major cities and 3-5 days for remote areas.

What's inside a build-a-box?

Build-a-Box lets you curate your own gift box from our selection of handcrafted chocolates, mini cakes, cookies, and floral add-ons. Choose 3-7 items to create your perfect gift.
